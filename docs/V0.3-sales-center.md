# V0.3 — Sales Center and referral network

## Affiliate experience

- `/dashboard/sales-center` is the entry point for targets, playbooks, messages, emails, call scripts, objections, product knowledge, assets, generated sales pages, favorites, the first-week plan, and funnel performance.
- `/dashboard/welcome` is a seven-step onboarding flow that can create the affiliate's first target-specific sales page.
- Public pages use `/a/{affiliate}/{target}`. Affiliates choose only approved content, CTA, target, language, and professional advisor details. Price and product claims always come from Studio ATS-controlled data.
- A lead is copied into the affiliate CRM only when the visitor explicitly accepts contact by the named advisor.

## Referral network

- `/dashboard/referrals` gives each affiliate a registration link and QR code.
- Registration records one immutable direct sponsor, resolved server-side from an approved affiliate code or slug.
- On an eligible sale, the seller receives 25% of the commission base and their direct sponsor receives an additional 5%.
- Only direct recruits generate sponsor commission. Recruits can sponsor their own direct network.
- Refunds, chargebacks, disputes, validation, payout, and attribution corrections keep both commission rows in sync.
- Existing commission rows keep their historical amount; the migration only changes future sales.

## Administration and controls

- `/admin/sales-content` manages versioned drafts and publishing. An active version is immutable; edits require a new version.
- `/admin/sales-pages` can unpublish or republish affiliate pages with an audit reason.
- `/admin/sales-analytics` reports content usage and the page/campaign funnel.
- Tenant RLS, service-only event writes, immutable sponsor assignment, cycle prevention, and catalog-owned prices are covered by SQL tests.

## Verification

- TypeScript, ESLint, Node unit tests, production build, and the local PostgreSQL harness must pass before release.
- The referral SQL test covers sponsor binding, the 25% + 5% split, exactly two commission rows, refund reversal, and sponsor dashboard isolation.
